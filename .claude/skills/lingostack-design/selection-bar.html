Concurrency is not parallelism

When people talk about goroutine scheduling, they often confuse two ideas. concurrency is not parallelism — concurrency is about dealing with many things at once, while parallelism is about doing many things at once.

A well-designed program can be concurrent without being parallel, which makes it easier to reason about and scale across cores.

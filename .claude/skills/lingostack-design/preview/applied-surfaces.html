Concurrency is not parallelism

When people talk about goroutine scheduling, they often confuse two ideas. concurrency is not parallelism — concurrency is about dealing with many things at once.

词条解释 · term modal

concurrency
/kənˈkʌrənsi/ · noun
名词
开发语境用法

在编程中，concurrency 常与 goroutine、thread、channel 同现。务必与 parallelism（并行）区分：并发是同时「应对」多件事，并行是同时「执行」多件事。
